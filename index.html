Continuing to mature as a profession

Challenges?

Growth in sports analytics has been rapid but uneven

  • Some sports / orgs have been faster to adapt / implement
  • Resourcing differences between sports and organisations within sports

Sometimes growing in ways we’re not quite ready for (“running before walking”)

  • E.g., focus on hiring data scientists / analytics specialists without understanding and investing in infrastructure and support functions upstream (IT, data engineering, data governance)
  • Varying maturity in leaders’ skills in using and interpreting data to inform their decisions

Role clarity and integration within a performance team

  • Looking for the mix of domain, interpersonal, data and analytics, and computer science / programming skills
  • Is analytics a distinct role? Is it better to upskill an SME? Is it just sport science with larger data sets?

Opportunities?

Many rich sources of data across disciplines, although the industry generally lacks sufficient mathematical / statistical knowledge to analyse in sophisticated and robust ways (“we don’t know what we don’t know”) – opportunities for those with specialist skill sets to make an impact

Volume of available data has grown rapidly, but have we lost some of our discernment in the process?
